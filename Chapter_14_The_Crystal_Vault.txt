Chapter 14: The Crystal Vault

The descent into the Obsidian Catacombs was colder than either Mira or Kael had anticipated. Frost clung to the volcanic rock, shimmering with the residual magic of the Ember Gate.

"Are you certain the Solarium Prism is down here?" Kael whispered, checking the tension of his bowstring.

Mira held up her lantern, its golden flame casting long shadows across the corridor. "The ancient codex in the library was explicit. When Lord Vane breached the northern seal, the guardians hid the third key inside the Crystal Vault."

They reached a massive archway framed in cerulean quartz. At the center stood a pedestal of white marble, upon which floated a radiant prism of pure crystalline light—the Solarium Prism.

Before Kael could take a step forward, a mechanical sentry stepped out from the darkness, its twin swords glowing red. "Halt, intruders," the sentry intoned. "Only one who bears the Royal Signet of Eldoria may claim the flame."
